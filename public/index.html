Upload your degree flowchart and get a semester plan you can drag around. It reads PDF, Excel and image files right in your browser, for free, and never lets a class land before its prerequisites.

Flowchart files
Drop files here, or tap to choose PDF, Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or PNG and JPG images. Several screenshots are fine. You can also paste a screenshot.

Tip: Excel and PDF files read best. For screenshots, zoom in so the text is sharp.

Drag a class to another semester (on a phone, hold the dotted grip). Green columns are allowed, red ones break a prerequisite. A class you move gets pinned, so "Auto suggest again" keeps it there; tap its pin to let it move again. Tap a class to light up its chain.
